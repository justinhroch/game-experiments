Supernova

A game by Dagny and Justin

Orion was born from the stars. A celestial being from the planet Picaeopus gave him his very first power. Now Princess Nova needs his help. Tyrantus, the evil king of Tytaculus, is sending his alien army through rips in space to take over her kingdom, Atalandia. Orion must team up with a dragon, master the four elements, free the dragons Tyrantus has taken over, and face the king himself in one huge final battle!

⭐

The Hero: Orion

Orion used to live an ordinary life. Then one day he explored the star and realized he was destined to save Atalandia. Soon after, Princess Nova came asking for his help. Orion has a starry origin. A celestial being from Picaeopus gave him his first power. He starts out small, but every battle and every bit of training makes him stronger.

👑

Princess Nova

Nova comes from Picaeopus too, and she's fighting to save her kingdom, Atalandia. She's a warrior and a teacher. She trains Orion and helps him learn to control his powers.

🐉

Train Your Dragon

Orion trains his dragon to use the four elemental powers. Each one you master unlocks new moves, new places to explore, and new ways to fight.

🔥 Fire
💧 Water
🪨 Earth
🌪️ Air
⚔️

Good Dragons vs. Possessed Dragons

Some dragons are on your side. Others have been possessed by Tyrantus and turned against everyone. You can't tell who's who until the fight begins!

💫

Free the Dragons

When you beat a possessed dragon, you don't destroy it. You set it free! The freed dragon thanks you by giving you its power, so every win makes your team stronger.

👽

The Bad Guys

  • Tyrantus is the cruel dark elf king of Tytaculus, and he wants to rule every world he can reach.
  • The Tyrenies are his minions. They're sleek dark elf soldiers with ashen skin, pointed ears and glowing eyes, and there are a LOT of them.
🌑

The Rift

There's no mothership. Instead, a space event like an eclipse keeps coming back, and each time it tears the fabric of space-time. When a rift opens, the Tyrenies teleport straight through. When you see the sky go dark, get ready!

⚡

The Battle for Atalandia

On the grassy sea cliffs of Atalandia, Orion, Nova and their dragons stand against Tyrantus and the Tyrenies as the eclipse rift opens overhead!

🔥

Final Boss: Tyrantus

Once you've mastered every element and freed the dragons, Orion and his dragon team take on Tyrantus in an epic showdown to save Atalandia for good.
